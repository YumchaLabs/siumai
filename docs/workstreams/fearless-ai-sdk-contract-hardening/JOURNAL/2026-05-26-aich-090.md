# AICH-090 Unsupported Capability Policy

Date: 2026-05-26
Status: DONE

## Work

- Compared Siumai's warning/error paths against AI SDK `SharedV4Warning` and legacy call-warning
  shapes.
- Added `UnsupportedCapabilityPolicy` and `UnsupportedCapabilityBehavior` to record reject, warn,
  and provider-fallback decisions before projecting to warnings or errors.
- Added core `resolve_unsupported_capability_policy(...)` so reject policies become
  `LlmError::UnsupportedOperation`, while non-reject policies produce response/stream warnings.
- Added `ProviderCapabilities::reject_if_unsupported(...)` and routed hard family guards for
  embedding, image, audio, files, and rerank through the shared reject policy.
- Documented the policy in the workstream design and updated root/spec/core changelogs.
