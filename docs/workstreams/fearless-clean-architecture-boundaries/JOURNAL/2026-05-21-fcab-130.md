# FCAB-130 Journal — Family Taxonomy Finalization

Date: 2026-05-21

## Scope

FCAB-130 settled the stable family taxonomy after the facade surface was tightened. The task
specifically asked whether Video should be treated as a stable family and whether Music should stay
extension-only.

## Source-backed decision

- Video is already source-backed as a stable family:
  - `siumai-core::video::VideoModel` and `VideoModelV4`
  - `siumai::video::*`
  - `siumai::prelude::unified::{video, VideoModel, VideoModelV4}`
  - `siumai_registry::registry::entry::VideoModelHandle`
  - `ProviderRegistryHandle::video_model(...)`
- Music remains extension-only:
  - `MusicGenerationCapability` is a capability trait, not a `MusicModel` family trait.
  - Registry has no `music_model(...)` handle and no `MusicModelHandle`.
  - Current compatibility support delegates music through the language handle / extension capability
    path.
